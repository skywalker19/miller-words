## 2. Accuracy decisions

- **pick up** has two common meanings. For this story, use “捡起”; teach “接送” separately later.
- **add balloons to toy trucks** is understandable but not a very natural real-life action. It is retained only because the word must appear. A later review sentence should use **add** in a more typical context, such as “Add three more blocks.”
- **boards** is safer as “木板” unless the picture clearly shows a diving board or balance board.
- **built monster** needs an adjective such as “newly built” to sound natural.
- **bow** should be learned in the phrase **take a bow**, with the pronunciation /baʊ/ rather than the ribbon pronunciation /boʊ/.
